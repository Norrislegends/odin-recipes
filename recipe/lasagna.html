Chef John's Lasagna recipe

Today we are making the delicious and infamous Chef John's Lasagna recipe!

A great lasagna only needs two things: a thick, rich, super-meaty sauce (and lots of it!) and a ricotta fillings
where only the finest cheeses are welcomed. For the sauce, I love a 50-50 combo of Italian sausage and lean ground beef.

ingredients:

Meat Sauce:

  • ½ pounds lean ground beef
  • 1 pound bulk italian sausage
  • 8 ounce package fresh mushrooms, coarsely chopped
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • ½ teaspoon freshly ground black pepper
  • ½ teaspoon Italian seasoning (Optional)
  • ¼ teaspoon red pepper flakes
  • 6 cups prepared marinara sauce
  • ¼ cup water, or as needed (Optional)

Pasta:

  • 1 (16 ounce) package lasagna noodles

Ricotta Filling:

  • 2 large eggs
  • 2 pounds whole-milk ricotta cheese
  • 8 ounces fresh mozzarella cheese, diced
  • ⅔ cup freshly grated Parmigiano-Reggiano cheese
  • ¼ cup chopped fresh parsley
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • ¼ teaspoon freshly ground black pepper
  • 1 pinch cayenne pepper, or to taste

Cheese Topping:

  • 4 ounces fresh mozzarella cheese, diced
  • ½ cup freshly shredded Parmigiano-Reggiano cheese

Directions:

  1. Boil the lasagna noodles.
  2. Cook the ground beef.
  3. Add tomato sauce to the beef.
  4. Mix ricotta with an egg.
  5. Spread meat sauce in a baking dish.
  6. Add a layer of noodles.
  7. Add ricotta and mozzarella.
  8. Repeat the layers.
  9. Top with mozzarella and Parmesan.
  10. Bake at 190°C (375°F) for about 40–45 minutes.
